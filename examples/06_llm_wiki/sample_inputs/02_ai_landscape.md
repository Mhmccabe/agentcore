# AI Company Landscape — 2024

## OpenAI

**OpenAI** was founded in 2015 as a non-profit by **Sam Altman**, **Elon Musk**, **Greg Brockman**,
and others, later transitioning to a capped-profit model. Their **GPT** model series, including
GPT-4 and GPT-4o, dominates enterprise AI adoption. OpenAI also produces:

- **DALL-E** — image generation
- **Whisper** — speech recognition

**Microsoft** invested heavily in OpenAI and integrated its models into **Azure OpenAI Service**
and **GitHub Copilot**.

## Google DeepMind

**Google DeepMind** was formed by the merger of Google Brain and DeepMind in 2023. They develop
the **Gemini** model family:

| Model | Target use |
|---|---|
| Gemini Ultra | Highest capability |
| Gemini Pro | Balanced capability/cost |
| Gemini Flash | Speed and efficiency |

Google distributes Gemini through **Google Cloud's Vertex AI** platform, competing directly
with Amazon Bedrock and Azure AI.

## Meta AI

**Meta AI** released the **Llama** model family as open weights, allowing developers to run
models locally or fine-tune them without API costs. Llama 3 models are available on Amazon
Bedrock, Azure, and Google Cloud, making Meta a cross-cloud AI provider despite not selling
AI services directly.

## Mistral AI

**Mistral AI** is a French AI startup founded in 2023 by former Google DeepMind and Meta
researchers. They produce efficient open and commercial models including:

- **Mistral 7B** — efficient open model
- **Mixtral** — mixture-of-experts architecture

Mistral models are available on Amazon Bedrock and Microsoft Azure.
